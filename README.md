### English

I am a freelance developer based in Brittany, France, working remotely. ClosiQode is my studio for custom websites and web tools, built for small and medium businesses.

The person who answers you is the person who writes the code.

**What I do**

- **Custom business applications**: replace spreadsheets, emails and copy-pasting with a tool built around the way you work.
- **AI assistants for your trade**: make your company's know-how searchable by your team.
- **Data extraction**: turn PDFs and documents into checked, structured data.
- **Automation**: connect your software so nothing is typed twice.
- **Showcase websites**: three mock-ups to compare before the site is built.

**Qlynk**

[Qlynk](https://qlynk.eu/en/) is the URL shortener I design and maintain: short links, QR codes, links that open the right app on mobile, bot-free click statistics and an API. Servers in France, no cookies for visitors. Available in English, French and Spanish.

**Open source**

- [qlynk-links](https://github.com/ClosiQode/qlynk-links): a skill for Claude.ai and Claude Code that drives a Qlynk account in plain language (links, QR codes, clicks, categories). Python 3, no dependencies, MIT license.
- [calc-ae](https://github.com/ClosiQode/calc-ae): a desktop calculator for the social and tax charges of a French auto-entrepreneur (in French), MIT license.
